DialogWright / Agent mode

Agent mode

A natural conversation, with what gets done still fixed. A model talks with your callers from one file that describes your business. Nothing is booked or sent until the line reads it back and the caller confirms.

How it works

  1. Describe the businessOne file: services, prices, hours, common questions and what callers can do. No call flow to design.
  2. The model talksIt answers in its own words and takes details in any order, like a person at the counter.
  3. DialogWright decidesEvery action is checked against your rules and logged. Nothing runs until the caller confirms.

What stays fixed

  • Read back in your wordsBefore anything is booked or sent, the line reads the details back exactly as they will be sent.
  • Only when the caller confirmsA separate model checks that the caller really confirmed. If it is not sure, nothing runs.
  • Your rules, in codeEvery action goes through a policy gate, and every decision is logged.
  • Nothing quietly droppedThe line will not end a call with a request still unconfirmed without asking the caller first.
  • No invented prices or timesPrices come only from your file, and times only from your calendar.
  • Honest about being automatedAsked if it is a person, it says it is the business's automated assistant.

Built for the phone

  • Interrupt any timeCallers can talk over any reply. The line stops and listens.
  • Confirmations are heardIf a caller talks over a read-back, the next one plays in full, so they know what they are confirming.
  • Patient with silenceAfter nine seconds of quiet, the line checks the caller is still there, and ends politely if no one is.
  • Knows your hoursIt greets after-hours callers accordingly and never promises a callback time you do not give.
  • One task at a timeOn a line that does several things, the model sees only the task the caller is on.
  • Phone and chatThe same app answers calls on Telnyx or Twilio, and a chat on your website.

It's a fine line between time to think and "did it hang up on me?"

What a call costs

  • $0.19a call on Telnyx, as billed, for the phone line, recognition and voice
  • $0.24a call on Twilio, as billed
  • Under 1¢a call for the models, in either mode
  • $0.005a minute for Krisp noise suppression on Telnyx, an option

The phone line is most of the cost, so choose the mode and the model for what the job needs.
