Who we are, and what this covers

DialogWright is phone and chat software made by Jason Stiles, a sole proprietor ("we" on this page). This page covers dialogwright.com and DialogWright's own demonstration lines: the phone numbers, chat pages and text messages we run to show and test the software, including the sample business Example Wheel Works.

Example Wheel Works is not a real shop. A request made on its line comes only to us, and no repair is carried out.

The DialogWright software is open source. A business that runs it on its own decides what its line records and is responsible for its own privacy notice; this page does not cover it.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Calls and chats with a demonstration line

When you call or chat with a demonstration line, we keep:

  • the number you call from, the number you called, and when;
  • what you said and what the line said, as text (the transcript), and how long each took;
  • the details you give for a request: your name, your car, the number to reach you on, whether we may text you, and any note;
  • a record of each action the line took or refused, such as a request sent.

The demonstration lines do not keep recordings of the audio of a call. Your voice is turned into text as you speak, by the carrier's speech recognition, and the line's replies are spoken by the carrier's voice.

We use this to answer you and do what you ask (send a request, leave a callback note, text you about it), and to test and improve the software: we read calls to find what the line got wrong. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or send you marketing.

Text messages

A demonstration line texts you only if you agree on the call. The line asks whether it may text you at your number, and reads your answer back to you with the rest of your request; nothing is sent unless you confirm it. The texts are about your request only: a request for a photo of the damage, a price, a booking confirmation, and a reminder before a booking. There is no marketing.

  • How many: it varies with your request, usually up to four texts for one request.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, and HELP for help. The full text message terms are on a page of their own.
  • Photos and messages you text us are kept with your request, as on a call.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, other than the providers that deliver the messages for us.

Who processes it

A call or chat passes through a few service providers, each only to do its part:

  • Phone carriers (Telnyx and Twilio): connect the call, turn speech into text and text into speech, and deliver text messages.
  • Language models (TypeSafe, Meta, and for some demonstrations models reached through OpenRouter): read the conversation to understand what you asked, and word the line's replies.
  • Our server: a computer we operate in the United States, where the records above are kept.

We do not give your information to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Records of demonstration calls, chats and texts are kept for as long as we need them to test and improve the software, and are deleted when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we keep about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing jason@dialogwright.com with the number you called from. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. The demonstration lines are not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, its effective date above changes with it. Questions: jason@dialogwright.com.
