What a call costs, and why it should not decide your design

By Jason Stiles, November 26, 2026

We've got two cheap but effective models: Jev, the decision model, and Muse Ember, the model that holds the conversation in agent mode. Ember costs more, but it's a rounding error on the telephony costs.

That means the right approach is using the model and the call type that's best for the job, not one dictated by price. Telnyx as the carrier, and the work we did so it could handle barge-in, is a bigger money saver against Twilio's fees than decision model against agent mode ever could be.

The numbers

  • $0.0084a call for agent mode's models, at most: Ember measured, the decision model an upper bound
  • $0.002a call for scripted mode's decision model, measured on one call
  • $0.19 and $0.24a call for the carrier, recognition and speech, as billed on Telnyx (the median) and on Twilio, the same in both modes
  • 2.6% to 3.2%of a call's total is the difference between the modes, about $0.006

At 2,000 calls a month the difference is about $12. It takes about 16,000 calls a month to reach $100. Telnyx with Krisp is billed $0.060 a minute against Twilio's $0.0785, about 24% less: on a call of three and a half minutes, about $0.065, about ten times the whole difference in model cost per call.

Both carriers carry the call through a conversation relay, their service that turns the caller's speech into text and the line's words into speech. Its price includes both.

Krisp is an option. It costs $0.005 a minute, about 8% of a Telnyx call: $0.015 to $0.02 on the wheel shop example's calls, billed by the call's whole minutes. It is one setting, TELNYX_NOISE_SUPPRESSION=krisp (the guide's 13.1), and you can leave it out. What it bought is not yet measured alone: the loud-room calls compared Telnyx, with Deepgram's flux recognizer and Krisp, against Twilio (10 speech events against 135, no reply cut off against 7), and the same room has not yet been called on Telnyx without Krisp. So that result is the Telnyx path with Krisp, not Krisp alone. That measurement is to come.

Measured: the models' tokens and cost from the records (traces) of 17 live agent-mode calls and one live scripted call, and what each carrier billed for the wheel shop example's test calls, read from their own records on 2026-11-26 (on both, the conversation relay's price includes recognition and the voice).
