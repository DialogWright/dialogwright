Telnyx crushed it in a loud room

By Jason Stiles, November 26, 2026

Holy cow. Telnyx just crushed it. I had a loud football game going on, as well as side conversations between my wife and son, and I called the wheel shop agent on Telnyx. It heard only me. Twilio was unusable in the same environment.

Flux and Krisp have completely switched my carrier preference. I was frustrated with Telnyx at first, for not being as good as Twilio, but switching to Deepgram's flux recognizer corrected that complaint about instant barge-in audio, and Krisp is a game changer for background noise.

What the two calls showed

The same room, the same app, one call on each carrier:

CarrierSpeech events that reached the lineReplies cut offOutcome
Telnyx, with Deepgram's flux recognizer and Krisp noise suppression100The line heard only me, and the request was sent in nine turns
Twilio, with flux, low interrupt sensitivity and backchannel ignored1357The room's voices were transcribed as my words, and the call could not go on

On Twilio I had on everything its own guidance offers for noise. Nothing else could be set to fix it: ConversationRelay has no noise suppression of its own, and DialogWright's server never gets the call's raw audio, so there is nothing on the server to filter. Telnyx denoises the caller's audio before its recognizer hears it.

What changed

Telnyx is now the recommended carrier, and Twilio stays fully supported. For an English app, pnpm configure writes these three when you choose Telnyx: 

TELNYX_TRANSCRIPTION_PROVIDER=deepgram
TELNYX_SPEECH_MODEL=flux
TELNYX_NOISE_SUPPRESSION=krisp

One thing this test does not tell apart: it compared Telnyx with Krisp against Twilio, so it shows what the whole Telnyx setup did, not Krisp alone. Next game I'm calling the same line with Krisp off, and I'll add what it does by itself here. Krisp costs half a cent a minute, about two cents on a call, and flux is for English only. Telnyx is also the cheaper carrier per call, which is a post of its own.
