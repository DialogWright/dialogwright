DialogWright / Text message terms

Text message terms

The terms of the text messages DialogWright's demonstration lines send. Effective November 26, 2026.

The program

DialogWright, made by Jason Stiles, runs demonstration phone lines for its software. One of them answers as Example Wheel Works, a sample wheel repair shop. Its texts are about a request you made on a call: a request for a photo of the damage, a price, a booking confirmation, and a reminder before a booking. They are not marketing.

How you agree

On the call, the line asks whether it may text you at your number. Your answer is read back to you with the rest of your request, and nothing is sent unless you confirm it. If you say no, we call you instead. Agreeing to texts is never a condition of a request or a purchase.

How often, and what it costs

  • Message frequency varies with your request: usually up to four texts for one request.
  • Message and data rates may apply, under your mobile plan.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping texts, and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts; you will get one message confirming it, and no more. Reply START to receive them again. Reply HELP for help, or email jason@dialogwright.com.

Privacy

What we keep and how it is used is on the privacy page. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
