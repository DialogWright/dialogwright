Example Town Library Events

Events at the library

Story time

Story time for young children is held every Tuesday and Thursday at 10 a.m. in the children's room of the main branch. Parents and carers stay with their children for the whole session, which lasts about forty minutes and ends with a song.

Book club

The book club meets on the first Monday of each month at 7 p.m. in the meeting room of the main branch. Each month the club reads one novel chosen by a vote at the previous meeting, and copies are held at the desk for anyone who wants to join.
