# testkit

testkit is the engine's own test fixture, not a demo and not a starting point for an app. Its domain is a neutral one, chosen so the engine's tests have an app that uses every hook in the contract and reaches its edge cases; its choices are made to exercise the engine, not as a model of how to design an app. To learn how to build an app on the engine, read the example apps (a clinic and a utility company).

Example Parcels is a small, fictional parcel delivery line. It has three forms: tracking a parcel (a read that needs the one-time code, a record chosen from the caller's own, scope by record), a delivery window (a read at level 1, a choice and a day ahead, R2 by account), and reporting a missing parcel (a confirmed write with a summary, R3, R5 for depot staff, an app rule R8, and a downstream service, the depot agent, that the call waits on). A customer verifies with an account ID and a date of birth; depot staff sign in and act for their depot's customers as a viewer or a clerk. The systems are a deterministic in-memory mock. All names, numbers and dates are made up (phone numbers are in the 555 range). Its regression fixtures are in `fixtures/` (`pnpm regress:testkit`), and `testkit.test.ts` checks that the app is valid, that its manifest has every line the engine can ask for, and that every scenario runs clean against the stub.
