## What worked well

- `pnpm create-app --identity` produced a green app in about a second, with a `src/app.ts` whose comments explain each hook.
- patterns.md is close to a recipe for this kind of paragraph: verification, a one-time code with a purpose, phone and chat, delegates with `principalEntry` and an entry-check, confirmed writes with one field list, `limit` with a lookup, the 30-day custom rule, `onSummaryRead`, and the policy tests. Nearly everything in `src/app.ts` and `app.test.ts` came from it with names changed, and it compiled first time.
- `pnpm check` messages are precise and their fixes were right every time.
- The policy matrix is the best review tool in the repository: reading it found a real hole (`sendCode` for a property manager) that neither the worksheet nor the tests had caught.
- The scenario `text` expectation (a substring of the last turn's words) made completion lines and refusals easy to pin down.
