# The console as a call-center dashboard: the build

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: **built: Console 1 and Console 2 (see As built).** Source: the maintainer, 2026-10-11, on a mockup: today the console opens on one call; a realistic console opens on the calls in progress, a click opens a call's detail as today, a menu leads to the console's other pages, with statistics, minutes and cost. His answers: supervisors will use it eventually (so a "take this call" action and console roles with single sign-on come later, not now); alerts yes; carrier cost from a per-minute rate per carrier, left out when no rate is set. It builds on Phase 8b (one console sees every server's calls) and several apps (every app's calls, with a filter).

## Pages

1. **Live, the home page: what is happening now.**
   - A strip of four numbers: calls in progress; waiting for a person or being transferred; today's share handled with no person (containment); today's cost.
   - The calls in progress, one row each: the app; the caller, masked as today; the channel and carrier; what the caller is doing (the task and the step); how long; and a status: on track, a second try (a slot past its first attempt), frustrated, transferring, with a person (handed off).
   - A click opens the call's detail, the view the console has today, live.
   - Alerts: a call that turns frustrated or starts waiting for a person is marked in the list, counted in the page title and, when the viewer has turned it on, makes a short sound. The sound is off by default and remembered per browser.
2. **Calls: what happened on a call.** Past calls from the traces (local files or the S3 store), newest first, searchable by the caller's last four digits, a date range, the app, the task and how the call ended (handled, to a person and why, hung up and where, an error), paged; a click opens the call's replay as today.
3. **Insights: how the line is doing.** For a date range and an app (or all):
   - calls, and calls by hour with the peak number at once;
   - containment: handled with no person;
   - transfers by reason;
   - abandonment by step: the last question a caller heard before hanging up;
   - where callers struggle: re-asks, silences and slots that ran out, per slot and per question;
   - task completion per intent: started against finished;
   - call length overall and per task;
   - minutes, the decision model's cost (exact, from the trace), the carrier's cost when a rate is set (`CARRIER_RATE_PER_MIN_TWILIO`, `CARRIER_RATE_PER_MIN_TELNYX`, in dollars per minute; left out entirely when unset), and cost per call and per handled call;
   - safety: the injection screen's hits and the gate's refusals by rule;
   - after-hours calls (routes on `hours`), and the knowledge base's unanswered questions (the count `kb:gaps` would rank, with the command to run).
   Figures are computed from the traces and cached per app and day, so a page does not read every trace every time; today's are recomputed as calls end.
4. **Knowledge: the knowledge base at a glance**, per app: passages by state (fresh, stale, edited, never approved, drafts) as `kb:status` reports them, and the top unanswered questions. Read-only: approving stays a person at their terminal or the local review page until console roles exist.
5. **Health: is the line healthy.** The servers seen in the last minutes (with a shared store, every server; else this one) and their calls; `/health`'s numbers (sessions, trace write failures, disk and its low-space warning); the decision model's latency (median and 95th percentile over the last hour); reconnects and fallback dials today; the audit verified on demand.

## Decisions

1. **Same server, same sign-in.** The console stays where it is (`/dashboard`), behind `CONSOLE_AUTH` (local or token) and its access log; every new page and data path is under it and recorded as today. Nothing new is reachable without a session.
2. **No build step, nothing from elsewhere.** The page stays the console's own HTML and script under its nonce Content-Security-Policy; charts are inline SVG drawn by its own script; no library from a CDN.
3. **Redaction as today.** Every new view shows only what the console already may: masked numbers, masked slots by their app's settings, no secrets. Aggregates hold counts and durations, never values.
4. **Phone width.** Every page works at 375 pixels: the list becomes cards, the strip wraps, charts scale.
5. **One app, no host:** the app column and filter are hidden; everything else is the same.
6. **Later, by decision:** taking over a call, console roles and single sign-on, approving passages in the console, exports.

## Items

Built in two items through the usual loop (a worktree, an implementer, a reviewer, the full local suite, a fast-forward onto local main):

- **Console 1: the frame, Live and Calls**, with the alerts and the call detail reached from both.
- **Console 2: Insights, Knowledge and Health**, with the daily figures cache and the carrier rates.

## As built (Console 1)

**The pages are one page.** `/dashboard` stays the one HTML page under its nonce policy, with client-side navigation by the address's fragment: `#/live` (the home page), `#/calls` (its filters in the fragment, so Back keeps them), `#/insights`, `#/knowledge`, `#/health`, a call's detail at `#/call/<id>` and its replay at `#/replay/<id>?app=<app>`. `#/call/latest` is the view that follows each new call as it starts, as `/dashboard` used to open (the Live list links to it as "Follow the newest call"), so a presenter keeps today's view. The fragment never reaches the server, so every data path is the same few routes behind the console session. Insights, Knowledge and Health are sections that say what they will show; each is an entry in the page's `PAGES` table, waiting for an `enter` of its own.

**The bus keeps every call.** It followed only the newest call (a second call's start dropped the first's history), which a list of calls in progress cannot be built on. It now keeps each call's history: every call in progress, and the newest twenty that ended (bounded at 500 calls, 2,000 events each). An event for a call it never saw start is still ignored. The idle sweep publishes the end of every evicted call, not only the newest's. The default live feed still follows the newest call, filtered on the server (on a host with `?app=`, the newest of that app's calls), so one machine's view is as it was; `?call=<id>` is one call's feed, for a call's detail.

**The Live list is worked out on the server.** Sending every call's every event to each viewer would send each call's whole history (questions, records) to a page that shows one line of it. `/dashboard/events?view=calls` sends every kept call's row, then each row that changed and each call gone; the rows are worked out once per bus (`server/dashboard/live.ts`) by the same function the page could use (`view.js callRow`), from the events the bus already carries, each in its own app's words. No new event field was needed: the status is read from the records (`gates`' frustration row, `slots[promptedFor].attempts`, a handoff decision) and the bus's `handoff` and `ended` events. A row holds the masked caller, the app's labels for what was asked, counts and times, never a value a caller gave.

**Statuses.** Frustrated is sticky for the call once the frustration gate fires (its first two rungs or its handoff), as the session's own count is. With a person is a call that ended in a handoff: the server never sees the person's leg, so the row stays two minutes (`HANDED_OFF_SHOWN_MS`) and leaves the list. Alerts are the frustrated, transferring and with-a-person rows; an alert is a change (the page's first list raises none).

**Calls: a summary read from the trace, kept in memory; nothing written.** The plan offered reading a call's first and last records, or a summary written when a call ends. Neither gives everything the page shows: how a call ended is in the frame log (its `end` frame, a closed socket, a chat's `ended`), not the records, and the cost is every record's. A summary file written at the end would need every end path (the adapter, the hangup webhook, the idle sweep, the chats) to write one, and nothing for calls made before it. So `server/dashboard/calls.ts` lists calls by name (a folder's files with their sizes and times; the store's day folders and segment names, the reader now narrowing by day), and reads a call's trace and frame log only for the page shown or, for a filter a name cannot answer, newest first until the page is filled, folding them as the page folds a replay (`replayEvents`, `callRow`), redacted as the replay route redacts them. Each summary is kept in memory for the server's run by the call's size and last write; one request reads at most 1,000 calls and says when it stopped there. Console 2's daily figures cache is where summaries would be kept across runs. The order is newest first by last write (what names give), and the date range is on each call's start. A call with no end recorded and nothing written for 30 minutes is shown as ended with "no end recorded" (an error, or to a person if a handoff was under way).

**Today's figures** (`/dashboard/today?since=<the viewer's midnight>`) come from the same summaries: containment is the share of today's ended calls no person took; the cost is the decision model's, every record's `usage.costUsd`.

**A feed's losses.** The page used gaps in the events' numbers to tell a stalled viewer it had missed events; a feed of one call now has gaps that are no loss (the numbers are the bus's, across calls). The server says it instead: an `event: missed` with the count before its next write, and the Live list sends the whole list again.

**Recorded as before.** Opening the Live list or a call's detail is opening the live feed (`live`, its first open in a session); a replay from the Calls page is a `replay` entry as every replay is; the Calls list, like the traces list, is a list and records nothing. Every new path answers 401 without a session (tested with the sign-in's other paths).

**Not done here.** The list is not virtualised (hundreds of calls in progress draw as hundreds of rows); the sound needs one touch of the page after a reload before a browser lets it play.

### Review fixes (Console 1)

- **No call in progress for ever.** A turn that finishes after the hangup webhook (one under way when it came) no longer makes the call live again, on the bus, the Live list or the call's detail, which marks it as a late turn; only a reconnect does. A call with no end recorded and no word for 30 minutes is settled as ended ("no end recorded", `view.js settledRow`, the same rule as the Calls page's) on the Live list and the page alike, and the bus takes it for ended (`markStale`, every minute while the list has a viewer): a server that stopped with calls on it, or a peer that died, leaves none behind.
- **Late joiners.** A reconnect now carries what a call is listed by (`call`: the masked caller, its start, channel, carrier and app), and the bus takes a reconnect for a call it never saw start as that call's start, so a server that restarts lists its resumed calls, and one that joins lists a call that moves to or reconnects on any server. A call that never reconnects is not listed by a server that did not see it start; a chat resumed after a restart publishes no reconnect, so it is not listed until it ends. Said in 14.14.
- **Bounds.** Calls handed off in the last two minutes are kept past the 20 ended calls while the list shows them; the history has a byte budget (64 MB of events' JSON by default, a constructor option), dropping the oldest ended calls first, then halving the oldest calls' histories; what a call's history lost is counted, and a viewer catching up on it gets `event: missed` first.
- **The Live list's work.** A call is folded again only for an event that can change its row; a turn's audit entries, a delivery note, the handoff summary, a keypress, a silence or an interrupt only move its last word's time. Folding incrementally was not simple (the row reads the whole view), so it was not done.
- **The Calls page.** Local candidates are narrowed both ways by their last write (from the range's start to a day after its end, the longest a call lasts), so an old day is found without reading newer calls. The traces are read with `fs/promises`, a few at a time with a `setImmediate` between batches (a 3,000-trace search blocked the loop at most 7 ms, from about 500 ms), one search per session at a time (429; the page waits its turn), and summaries are kept in an LRU of 20,000, which is also how a search that stopped at its 1,000 reads goes on when repeated ("search again to continue"). Today's figures are kept per app and day start, worked out again when the bus sees a call end or after a minute.
- **Containment** is now the calls handled to the end with no handoff, of the calls that ended; hang-ups and errors are counted apart (`hungUp`, `errors`) for Insights.
- **The access log** records the first open of each call's detail per session (`live`, under the call's id, with its app on a host) and each search by a caller's last four (`calls_search`, with the last four and the range as ISO times).
- **The page** keeps "missed N events" until it is reloaded (a reconnect's catch-up skips what the view already passed); its lists have `cell` and `columnheader` roles, the menu `aria-current`, and each page a hidden heading that focus moves to on navigation.
- **Two streams per tab** (the list's and a call's) were kept rather than multiplexed: the guide's 14.9 says the HTTP/1.1 limit (six per server, so three tabs on a plain connection).

## As built (Console 2)

**Insights, Knowledge and Health are the frame's three pages,** each an `enter` in the page's `PAGES` table: Insights keeps its range and app in the address (`#/insights?range=7d&app=<id>`), Knowledge its days (`#/knowledge?days=30`), and Health asks again every 15 seconds while it is shown. Their data paths are four routes under the console's session: `/dashboard/insights?from=<ms>&to=<ms>[&app=]`, `/dashboard/knowledge[?days=7|30|90][&app=]`, `/dashboard/health` and `/dashboard/health/audit`. Every one answers 401 without a session.

**What a call adds is read with its summary.** The Calls page already read each call's trace once and kept its summary (Console 1). The same read now works out what the call adds to Insights (`figures.ts` CallFacts: each task it worked on and whether and when it finished; per question asked, its re-asks, silences and running out; the gate's refusals by the failing rule, and its step-ups apart; the screen's hits; routes taken by a rule reading `hours`; the knowledge base's gaps by kind and nearest topic) and keeps it beside the summary in the same LRU. Those are summed by the UTC hour each call started in (`HourFigures`), with the most calls at once in each hour among the day's calls (a call that started the day before is not counted in the next day's peak: noted, not fixed).

**The daily figures cache** (`figuresCache.ts`) keeps a day's `HourFigures` per app beside the traces: on one machine `<TRACE_DIR>/.figures/<day>.json` (each app's trace folder on a host), on the store `<prefix><day>/.figures/figures.json`, under the day folder as asked. A dot names the folder, so neither the Calls page, nor `kb:gaps`, nor the store's call listing takes it for a call; the store's retention sweep removes it with the day's folder, and the local sweep removes a day's file once every trace that could be that day's is past `TRACE_RETENTION_DAYS`. Each file has `FIGURES_VERSION` and the fingerprint of the traces it was worked out from (`<calls>:<bytes>:<newest write>`): on the store, the day folder's calls; on one machine, the files last written from the day's start to a day after its end, the same narrowing the Calls page uses. That window includes the next day's calls, so a day is worked out again while the following day has calls coming in; it reads only the calls not already in memory, and the files of a quiet day are read back as they are after a restart. Today's figures follow Console 1's counter of calls the bus saw end: a day's figures are taken from memory for a minute while none has ended, and looked at again as soon as one has. One computation per app and day at a time (a second asker waits on the first's promise); reads are `fs/promises` or the store's reader, a few at a time with a `setImmediate` between batches; a file that cannot be written is worked out again next time. A range is at most 92 days; its hours are those from the hour its start is in to its end, so a viewer's day is exact in any time zone whose offset is whole hours (a half-hour zone's range starts half an hour off: said in 14.9). Unlike the Calls page there is no 1,000-read bound: a day's figures need every call of the day; the first read of a long range on a busy line takes time, without holding up a call, and is kept.

**Redaction.** A figures file and every answer hold counts, durations and dollars, keyed by what the app is made of (form, slot, rule, handoff reason, topic, carrier) or by the app's console labels for where a call stood (the hang-up's "task · step"). The gaps keep no words: the Knowledge page shows the unanswered questions by nearest topic with counts and the latest time, and points at `pnpm kb:gaps` for the callers' words. Tests assert no caller number, account digits or spoken words reach a file or an answer.

**The gaps moved into the engine.** `kb:gaps`' collection (a trace record read as a turn, and what counts as a gap) was in `packages/kb-author`, which the engine may not import. It moved, unchanged, to `packages/dialogwright/src/kb/gaps.ts`, exported from `'dialogwright'` (`collectGaps`, `gapTurnOf`, `gapTurnsOfLines`, the types as `Gap*`); kb-author re-exports it, and its 35 tests still pass on it. The report's grouping and its fixes stay in kb-author. The console counts with the app's topic slots and informational intents from the running app, and the keyword retriever over its knowledge base for the nearest topic, as `kb:gaps` does.

**The carrier's cost.** `CARRIER_RATE_PER_MIN_TWILIO` and `CARRIER_RATE_PER_MIN_TELNYX` (dollars a minute, 0 to 100, checked at start, said in the settings line; 14.12, each `.env.example`). Unset, no carrier cost appears anywhere. The trace did not name a call's carrier, and the Calls page's rows always had none, so the adapter now logs it once in the frame log at each call's setup (`{ provider }`), and the summary reads it. A call recorded before that names no carrier: its minutes are counted as `unknown` and not priced (said on the page). Each voice call is billed as carriers bill, its length rounded up to the minute, at least one; Insights shows minutes exact beside it. The Live strip's cost adds the carrier's to the model's when a rate is set.

**Struggles, per question.** "Per slot and per question" is one table keyed by what was asked (`promptedFor`: a slot by its console label, what the caller wants, a confirmation, the one-time code). A re-ask is a slot asked again after an answer that did not fill it (its attempts counted up); the intent, a confirmation or a code asked again could not be told apart from being asked anew (after an informational answer, say), so for those only silences and running out are counted. A silence counts on the question asked before it; running out is a slot's `slotExhausted`, or a handoff for `max-attempts` on the question being asked.

**After hours.** The trace's routes say which subjects a rule read, not their values, so "after-hours calls" is the calls a route reading `hours` sent somewhere, by the point and what followed (a rule `hours: closed` and one `hours: open` both count, each under its outcome).

**Knowledge** counts the passages from the knowledge base as this server loaded it (what its callers hear now), as `kb:status` counts them (fresh with its approval logged, fresh but approved outside `kb:approve`, source changed, edited, never approved); drafts and proposed topics are counted in `kb/pending` when the server knows the app's folder: a host's apps (their `dir`), or, on a server of one, the folder it runs in when it holds an `app.yaml` (`pnpm start` and `pnpm --filter <app> serve` both run there). Read only; the page says approving stays at the terminal or the review page.

**Health.** The servers seen: with `SESSION_STORE=postgres`, each server publishes a `server` notice every 30 seconds and as it starts listening (its name, calls, chats, and today's reconnects and fallback dials: numbers only, each read back as a number), and answers a server heard from for the first time with its own, so a newcomer lists everyone at once; a server not heard from for two minutes leaves the list. On one machine, this server alone. `/health`'s numbers come from the same function `/health` answers with. The model's latency is the median and 95th percentile of every ask (a turn record with questions, `timing.askMs`) the bus carried in the last hour, watched from the server's start (`latencyOf(bus)` in index.ts). Reconnects and fallback dials are counted in http.ts as each callback is answered (`reconnect:*`; any `dial:*` or `gave-up`, and a new call put through while draining or whose token could not be saved), by `TIMEZONE`'s day, per server. The audit is verified by `/dashboard/health/audit`: the day files read with `fs/promises` and walked yielding every thousand entries (`verifyChainAsync`), or the database (`verifyPostgresAudit`, which now yields every thousand rows as it walks); one at a time, a second asker getting the running one's answer; an error's message has the database's password masked; the last result is kept for the page.

**The access log.** A page of figures is recorded the first time a session reads it (`insights`, `knowledge`, `health`, under `console`): they show counts, not calls, so a new range or a refresh is not. Every verification of the audit asked for is recorded (`audit_verify`), before it runs. A refused range (400) is not a read.

**Charts.** Inline SVG made by `view.js` (`barsSvg`, `splitSvg`), stretched to their box (`preserveAspectRatio="none"`) with their labels in the page's own HTML, so text never scales; every mark has a `<title>` (its number on hover), each chart has `role="img"` and a label, and a table view (`<details>` As a table). One series per chart, in the console's accent; the endings bar uses the console's own state colors with a legend that names and counts each part. CSP unchanged: nothing loaded from elsewhere. A test parses the page's script as a module (`node --check`), as a name declared twice left every page blank in the first browser check.

**Checked in a browser** (the built-in browser, a dev server over 63 scripted calls in a host of two apps and in one app): all three pages at desktop width and at 375 pixels (no page-wide horizontal scroll; wide tables scroll inside their card), the app filter on a host and its absence with one app, the range picker, the audit verified from the button, and the Live strip's carrier cost.

**Not done here.** The peak at once does not count calls carried over from the day before; a day's file is rewritten while the following day has calls; Insights for a long range is not streamed (the page says "reading…" until the whole answer comes).

### Review fixes (Console 2)

- **A decision for the maintainer's review (made by the coordinator while he was away): a closed day is final.** A day is closed once its traces can no longer change (on one machine once its end and a call's longest length, a day, have passed; on the store once it is before yesterday, UTC) and no call of it is still unsettled. From then its figures file is its record: never listed again, never worked out again when its traces are trimmed, swept or gone (the file is read even when no trace of the day is left). So figures outlive their traces, which they may, as they hold only counts and durations, under a retention of their own: `FIGURES_RETENTION_DAYS` (unset: kept; checked at start; 14.9, 14.12, each `.env.example`). The traces' retention no longer touches them: on one machine the sweep leaves `.figures`, and on the store `sweep` and `trim` skip the day's `.figures` folder (it had been taken for a call), and `sweepFigures` removes a day's figures by the new setting. This replaces "retention removes them with their day" and "a day is recomputed when a retention removed one" above. A file of an older `FIGURES_VERSION` (now 2) is worked out again while its day's traces are there; with them gone, a version-1 file is read as older figures (its peak by the hour kept, where callers hung up in its own words, its routes with neither open nor closed) and the page lists those days, and a file that cannot be read is left out and listed as unreadable.
- **Fallback dials.** Every dialog-decided transfer had counted as a fallback dial (any `dial:` note). The callback's answer now says so itself (`ActionAnswer.fallback`: closing, a store it could not read or reach, a session it cannot read, past its reconnects, a token it could not save; and a new call while draining), and only those are counted; tested with dialog-decided handoffs of three reasons.
- **A stale result under a newer fingerprint.** A second asker that listed newer traces waited on the first computation and kept its figures as its own. The work is now keyed by day and fingerprint (and whether it can close), and each keeps the figures of the traces it listed.
- **A call with no end recorded** stayed in progress in a day's file for good. The file now says when the latest such call will be taken for ended (`settleBy`, its last word and half an hour); the day is not closed and is worked out again after then.
- **The event loop.** A range's local candidates were filtered once per day (92 times their number); each is now put in its days in one pass (the day of its last write and the one or two before it whose window holds it), on the store each day asked is listed on its own, and only the days still open (or closed days not yet worked out) are listed at all.
- **The audit in Postgres** was read whole into memory to verify it. It is now read a page at a time by each table's key (keyset paging, 10,000 rows), an anchor's entry and a roster entry's anchor looked up a page at a time, keeping per chain only its newest intact seq and the newest an anchor names: memory grows with the calls and days, never the entries (`pnpm audit:verify` uses it too). Tested on PGlite with 480 entries in pages of seven: no answer larger than the page asked for, the same verdicts, breaks included.
- **Files checked as read**: every number finite and not negative, every map of its shape, every hour key an hour, spans in pairs; anything else is worked out again (or, for a closed day with no traces, unreadable). The page escapes every figure it puts into HTML.
- **The peak at once** is now worked out from each call's span (start and end, kept in the day's file) of every app together, over the range's hours; a call ending the millisecond another starts is not at once with it; a call already going when the range began (one that started before its first day) is not counted, said on the page and in 14.9. This replaces "the peak does not count calls carried over from the day before".
- **The carrier's cost** is said plainly (14.9, 14.12, `.env.example`) to be the automated part of the inbound leg only (time with a person after a transfer and the transfer's dialed leg are not seen); the Live strip names minutes on a carrier with no rate as unpriced, as Insights does.
- **Inputs and names.** `?app=` naming an app the host does not run answers 400 on Insights and Knowledge; the Knowledge page is read one at a time per session (429), as Insights is, each page apart. "After-hours calls" is now "calls routed on business hours" (`hoursRouted`), each route by its point, whether its rule asked for open or closed (read from the app's routes.yaml by the rule's number), and what followed; a named schedule's rule (`hours.<name>`) counts too. Where a caller hung up is kept by ids (`<form>|<asked>`, `greeting`, `between`, `after:<form>`) and said in the app's current console labels when shown.
- **Memory.** Each day kept is its sums, its calls by the hour and its calls' spans (2,000 app-days); its figures by the hour, which a range starting or ending inside it needs, are kept for 64 days and otherwise read back from its file.
- **Docs.** CLAUDE.md describes `server/dashboard/` once; the gap exports from `'dialogwright'` are in the guide's 12.
