# A branded chat page for every app

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: **decided (the maintainer, 2026-10-18); built on main.**

## The problem

Each app with a chat page wrote its own: a booking line's (300 lines), a knowledge-base demo's, an insurer's portal. Most of each was the same machinery: the route and its local-or-public switch, the chat's address, the connection's status, a sign it is thinking, starting over, formatted lines and answer buttons, accessibility and safety. Only the brand and the content around the conversation differed. Every app should have a branded page without writing one.

## The decision

Two layers.

1. **The conversation, full size, in the widget package**: `DialogWright.mountConversation(element, options)`. The conversation as a page's main thing, from the booking line's page with its brand taken out: the line's words large and plain, earlier ones dimmed, the person's beside them, thinking, ways to start (`starters`), answer buttons, formatted lines, a status, Start over, a new conversation after one ends. A shadow root on a `dialogwright-conversation` element, every word a text node, themed with `--dw-*` custom properties. Any page can host it: an app's own page, or a business's existing site.
2. **The app's chat page, the engine's**, from app.yaml for an app that writes no HTML: at `/<app id>/chat`, `brand` (name, `tagline`, `logo`, `colors`) above the conversation, `chat.page`'s `intro` (the chat formatting), `starters` (by default the labels of the app's forms and informational intents, at most six) and `phone` (env.<NAME>, a "Prefer to talk?" button). `serve: off | local | public`, off by default; the app's own `CHAT_PAGE` setting overrides it (off after a demo). It needs CHAT=on and WIDGET=on, said at startup where one is missing. An app's own page at that path wins.

- **One brand.** `brand.colors.accent` is the console's accent and mark too.
- **Safety.** Every value is escaped into the page; a colour is only a checked hex code; the intro is the chat formatting drawn as elements; the page has a strict content security policy; an SVG logo is served sandboxed. `pnpm check` checks the logo (type, presence, at most 512 KB) and the intro.
- **Not a site builder.** No service cards, images or layouts in YAML: an app that wants more writes its own page around layer 1, a few dozen lines of HTML and CSS.

## As built

The booking line's page was rebuilt on `mountConversation`: its mountains, wordmark and service cards stay its own; its conversation code went.
