# The knowledge base in agent mode, measured (2026-11-27)

The wheel shop's knowledge base ([18.15 of the guide](../../guide/18-agent-mode.md#1815-the-knowledge-base)) has five
topics, approved by Jason Stiles: aftercare and diamond cut (`risk: low`, the model may reword them), and the refinish
guarantee, sensors and balancing, and cracked or bent wheels (given as written, by instruction only). The model looks
an answer up with the runtime's `look_up`: the retriever (hybrid, the static embedder, floor 0.25, cap 3), then the
decision model's reading of which topic answers the question.

**The run.** Four simulated callers, four calls each, by voice and by chat: 32 calls. Three asked a question a topic
answers (aftercare, the guarantee, a cracked wheel); one asked something none does (card payments). The calls were
read by hand.

| Caller | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The guarantee | 8 of 8 gave the approved passage word for word |
| A cracked wheel | 8 of 8 word for word |
| Aftercare (may reword) | 7 of 8 accurate; one drift (below) |
| Card payments (no topic) | 8 of 8 said the shop has no answer it can give, and offered a callback note or a request |

**Past the passage.** Callers asked what the passages do not say (is a touchless car wash all right?). Each time the
model said the guidance does not cover it and offered a callback note; two callers took one.

**The drift.** One voice call: a caller asked, in effect, whether brush car washes were only to be avoided in the first
week, and the model agreed. The passage says to avoid them after the first week too. A leading question on a topic the
model may reword, once in 8.

**Speed and cost.** The first sentence at the median 0.83 s and at p90 1.31 s by voice, with lookups (0.72 s at the
median before the knowledge base). The conversation model cost $0.0035 a call.

**Lead-ins.** Before each lookup the model says a short line of its own ("Let me check what we recommend for aftercare"),
said at once, so the lookup has no dead air.

## Decided (Jason Stiles, 2026-11-27)

- **The drift:** aftercare stays `risk: low`; measure more before changing it.
- **The lead-ins stay:** the model's own short lines are better than a fixed hold line.

## Later runs, and a judge after speaking (2026-11-27)

**More drift.** The runs made for the runtime's `unanswered_question` (80 more calls of the same callers) were read for
drift too. Across all 112 calls there are six clear drifts, not one. Four are in one voice call: after a run of leading
restatements ("so no car washes at all after the first week?"), the model agreed that no car wash at all, touchless
included, is safe after the first week; the passage rules out car washes with brushes only. One is on the guarantee,
given as written: asked "you'll fix it at no charge?", the model began "That's right on the guarantee", where the
passage says nothing of a charge. Five of the six followed a caller's restatement that widened or changed the
guidance, and five were in a turn with no `look_up`, answering a follow-up from a passage looked up earlier.

**A judge after speaking, measured.** The idea: the decision model reads each reply after a lookup against its
passage with the knowledge-answer drift judge (the scripted engine's, before speaking), and a reply read as drift is
sent back to the model to correct itself on the next turn. On 246 labelled replies from these calls and 40 written
to test it:

- at the judge's default bar it flagged 225 of 236 faithful replies, about 91 needless corrections in 100 answers; at
  0.9, 17 in 100, catching half the drifts;
- the false alarms come from what the judge counts as added: a lead-in, a question back, an offer of a price or a
  callback, each nearly always in an agent's reply (the passage word for word with a lead-in scored 0.83);
- each sentence judged alone did worse, and a question in general terms in place of the caller's words did little;
- the judge took 85 ms at the median, so time would not rule out judging before speaking: accuracy does.

**Decided (Jason Stiles, 2026-11-27):** not worth building. If something is needed later, the first try is a
narrower question for the decision model (whether the reply contradicts the passage or adds a fact on the caller's
question, leaving offers and lead-ins alone), or several questions, each for one kind of drift, scored on the same
labelled replies. The labelled replies and the judge's cassette are kept outside the repository with the runs.
