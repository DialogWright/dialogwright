# The acknowledgement opener on three models

The maintainer's opener ([the design](../2026-11-11-agent-runtime.md#decided-by-the-maintainer-2026-11-11),
item 5) is `persona.opener: acknowledge` in agent.yaml. The system prompt asks the model to begin each
reply with a brief, natural acknowledgement that commits the business to nothing, varied, and left out where
it would sound odd. The aim was that the first whole sentence would reach the caller almost as soon as the
first word.

Three models of [the comparison](2026-11-model-comparison.md) were run again on the same 12 simulated
callers, 5 runs each:

- Claude Haiku 5.5 at effort low;
- Muse Ember 1.0 at effort minimal;
- GPT-6 Luna at effort minimal.

Each ran on `apps/wheelshop-agent` as it is, with `--opener acknowledge`, and was judged by Claude Sonnet 5.5
under the material-claims rubric. Each is compared with its own comparison calls,
[re-judged by Sonnet](2026-11-model-comparison-rejudged.md) under the same rubric, so the comparison is like
for like. Spent: $4.99 ($1.73, $1.68 and $1.58).

## What it shows

- **The opener hardly moves the first sentence.** On these models the time to the first sentence is mostly
  the time to the model's first word, which the opener cannot shorten. The model's first event, its median
  and p95, from the traces:

  | Model | Before | With the opener |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Haiku | 768 ms, 1992 ms | 864 ms, 1983 ms |
  | Ember | 644 ms, 1225 ms | 611 ms, 1248 ms |
  | Luna | 1415 ms, 4021 ms | 1533 ms, 3709 ms |

  - **Ember.** Its first sentence already came about 70 ms after its first event, so there was nothing to
    gain.
  - **Haiku.** The gap was larger at the slow end. The opener cut its p95 by about half a second, to 51 ms
    over the bar, and left its median where it was.
  - **Luna.** It stays far over the speed bars, with or without the opener.
- **How far each model took it up.** The count is of replies that begin with a sentence of six words or
  fewer:
  - **Luna** most: half its replies, against a fifth before. The most common openers were "Got it.",
    "You're welcome." and "I understand.", with 76 distinct ones.
  - **Haiku** more than before, mostly "Got it." (27 times in 338 replies), and three times the prompt's own
    example word for word.
  - **Ember** hardly at all: about the same share of replies as before.

  No model said the prompt's examples over and over.
- **Material claims fell** for Haiku (19 to 7) and Ember (16 to 10), and Luna held at 7 against 8. The
  opener asks for nothing on claims, and these runs are 60 calls each. Read this as run-to-run variation
  until a second run shows it again.
- **Ember's timeouts** went from none to 1.6% of turns: six slow requests from Meta's service, past 6 s.
  This is the provider's tail, not the opener's doing, but it now misses the timeout bar by 0.6 points.

## Decision: no model clears, so Mercury stays

No model clears every bar with the opener either, so `apps/wheelshop-agent` keeps `inception:mercury-3` at
effort `minimal`, with no opener. What each run missed, and by how much:

| Model, with the opener | Bars missed |
| --- | --- |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 (low) | first sentence p95 51 ms over; cooperative calls done correctly 25 points under (70%); unsupported material claims 7 over |
| Muse Ember 1.0 (minimal) | timeouts 0.6 points over (1.6%); cooperative calls done correctly 30 points under (65%); unsupported material claims 10 over |
| GPT-6 Luna (minimal) | first sentence median 910 ms over; p95 3578 ms over; timeouts 16.2 points over; cooperative calls done correctly 25 points under (70%); unsupported material claims 7 over |

The quality bars are now the ones every model misses:

- **Cooperative calls done correctly.** The best model is at 80% (Sonnet, re-judged), and no fast model is
  above 70%.
- **Material claims.** Every model has some.

The re-judged comparison names what most of them are: invented drop-off process, and a note or a window said
to be more than the actions recorded.

## The runs

```sh
pnpm eval apps/wheelshop-agent --personas apps/wheelshop-agent/eval/personas.yaml --runs 5 --parallel 5 \
  --model <model> --effort <effort> --opener acknowledge --judge anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5 --budget 3
```

The models were `anthropic:claude-haiku-5-5` at `low`, `meta:muse-ember-1.0` at `minimal` and
`openrouter:openai/gpt-6-luna` at `minimal`. The caller was Claude Haiku 5.5, as in the comparison. The first
sentence is timed over the chat wire, as there.
