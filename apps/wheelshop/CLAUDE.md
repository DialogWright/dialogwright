# Example Wheel Works: notes for AI coding assistants

Example Wheel Works is the DialogWright demo of **agent mode**: a fictional wheel repair shop's line, where a model leads the conversation from the shop's facts and the engine decides what is done. The line speaks as the business ("we", "the shop"), never as a named person (the maintainer's call 44). A dealership account, recognized by the number it calls from, books cars straight into a drop-off window; any other caller sends the shop a request, which it quotes from a photo; a caller who wants to speak with someone leaves a callback note. Read the repository's root `CLAUDE.md` first for the rules, then this folder's `DESIGN.md` (the demo's assumptions and every choice) and [the agent-mode design](../../docs/designs/2026-11-08-agent-mode.md) before changing anything.

## How an agent-mode app differs

- **One file is the app.** `business.yaml` holds everything about the shop the line may use: who it is and how it sounds (`business.voice`, a friendly, professional front desk; the owner's sample replies in `business.examples`), its services, what it doesn't do, its claims, the price line, where it is, its days and windows, its accounts, what a request, a booking and a callback note collect (each field a library slot type with a fixed question), each read-back and done line, the handoff, the closing and the owner's summary. The engine builds the intents, forms, slots, prompts, policy and identity from it. There is no `intents.yaml`, `forms.yaml`, `prompts.yaml`, `slots.yaml`, `policy.yaml` or `identity.yaml`, and `pnpm check` refuses one beside `business.yaml`.
- **The model proposes, the engine decides.** The model (style.yaml's `agent`: Mercury 3 on Inception at `instant`) understands the values and words the replies. Each value is checked by its field's slot type; a value a check refuses asks its field again, counting no attempt the first two times in a row; a text field's figures are not held to the caller's words (`figures: free`, the read-back is the guard; DESIGN.md says why none of the shop's needs `cited`); each reply is held to the facts by the checks (no figure or web address the facts don't hold, one question, the word budget, ending as a sentence) and the drift judge, and replaced by the field's fixed question when one fails. The read-backs, the done lines, the price line, the handoff and the closing are always said in `business.yaml`'s words. Every action goes through the gate, only after the caller says yes to the read-back. The model never calls a tool.
- **The conversation is sent to the agent model** (the maintainer's decision 1): so no field may be an identity factor (no `birthdate`), and no `text` field may be `length-summarized`.
- **Tool results the model sees** (`sees: availability` on each form's `day`; decision 2): the day and its free windows, checked in a closed form. A tool whose result the model sees never has a `text()` check.

## The folder

- `business.yaml`: the shop's facts. Each demo assumption is marked `# assumption: the owner corrects these`; DESIGN.md lists them.
- `app.yaml` (`mode: agent`, `testing: { adversarial: on }`), `style.yaml` (the agent model and the reply's word budget on a call).
- `policy.matrix`, `POLICY.md` and `APP-MAP.md`: what the engine built, read back, generated from `business.yaml`; `src/app.test.ts` fails when one differs from what the app generates.
- `src/shopPage.ts`: the shop page, `/wheelshop/shop` (`code.pages`): the requests, bookings by day and window, texts sent and received and callback notes, with the numbers whole and the photos as thumbnails, rendered by the server with no script. A console page (`auth: 'console'`): local only, or with `CONSOLE_AUTH=token` behind the console's sign-in; never public. Mounting it opens the shop's log. `src/shopLog.ts`: that log, `SHOP_DATA_DIR/shop.jsonl`, append-only JSON lines (every record and each write's key, each with its time), read back as the server starts; unset, the records are in memory, as in the tests and the regressions. `src/shop.test.ts` holds the round trip, the page and its access. DESIGN.md's "The shop page" has every choice.
- `src/quotes.ts`: quote and confirm (DESIGN.md "Quote and confirm"): the shop page's "Quote and book" form (`POST /wheelshop/shop/quote`, its token, once for the request and version), which holds a window with room and texts the customer `texts.quote`; the customer's reply, read by the decision model (`ctx.decide`, typed questions, never the word matched), which books the window or goes to the shop; and the hold's lapse (6 pm the day before, or 24 hours), on the page route's minute sweep, as the page is drawn and before a reply is read. `src/quotes.test.ts` holds it, with a fake decision model; no scripted call drives it.
- `src/app.ts`: the tools, each a stand-in. `availability` (the stand-in calendar, two windows a weekday with business.yaml's capacity, Friday morning full from a standing drop-off; its reply checked closed), the writes `request`, `book` and `callback`, and the texts `send_photo_link` (to the caller, only with their yes to `textOk`: the gate's `when`, never this code), `notify_owner_request` and `notify_owner_booking` (to the shop's phone, the setting `SHOP_PHONE`), sent through the server's sender (`ctx.texts`, `TEXT_FROM`) and printed where there is none; and `onText`, which sends a text that comes in on to the shop's phone, a photo from a number with a recent request with a line naming it and a thank-you to the caller (DESIGN.md, "Texting"). Every write is idempotent on `ctx.idempotencyKey` and declares a reply `check`. The code reads `business.yaml` with `loadBusiness` for the windows' capacity, the days, the accounts, the owner's summary and the texts' words (`texts`), so a change to the file is never a change to code.
- `fixtures/scenarios/` (`demo.json`: the two demo calls and a correction at the read-back; `focus.json`: the build plan's review focus, a price pushed, a claimed account, a full window, a request said over the greeting, a callback note, a caller who says no to texts and gets none, `consent-declined`, and the live calls' turns, `live-call-sorento`, `live-call-road-rash`, `live-call-friday-morning`, `live-call-porsche`, `live-call-infinity` and `live-call-bent-wheel`, with `no-at-anything-else` and `never-mind-cancels`). The calls whose middle turns would depend on the order the model asks in (`dealer-trade-ins`, `correction-at-read-back`, `retail-bmw`, `window-full`, `claimed-account`, `consent-declined`) answer what the line asks (`answers`, by the question: `request_day`, `readback_request`, a list for a question answered in turn), after their opening `steps`. Each spoken step names the agent's answer (`agent`: its reply, its values with the caller's words they came from, its action, `fromFacts`, `fixedLine`; a list where the turn asks again, shown the day's free windows), and each call holds properties (`values`, `action`, `said.notFigure`, `readBackSaid`, `nothingDoneBefore`), never lines. `fixtures/corpus.jsonl` is the decision model's alone: what it reads on every turn (the request at the opening, the yes or no at a read-back, the offer of the number calling). It runs with an agent that understands nothing (never the agent model), so a line answering a field records the field's question again: the values are the agent's, in the scenarios. `fixtures/expected/` is the stub baseline, made once and reviewed. `fixtures/recorded/` is the recording (Jev's cassette and the agent cassette, `agent/inception__mercury-3.instant.realtime.jsonl`), which `regress --client recorded` replays; DESIGN.md's "Recordings" is its triage. `fixtures/judge/values.jsonl` holds labelled text values for the agent's value judge (`pnpm render:judge-eval --cases apps/wheelshop/fixtures/judge/values.jsonl --replay` tables its recording). `fixtures/agent/` is the bake-off's (`cases.yaml` and its recording); `pnpm agent:bakeoff --cases apps/wheelshop/fixtures/agent/cases.yaml --models inception:mercury-3:instant:realtime --examples on --repeat 3 --judge-ms 150 --replay` tables it with no key.

## Commands

Run these at the repository root.

```sh
pnpm check
pnpm --filter @dialogwright/example-wheelshop typecheck
pnpm --filter @dialogwright/example-wheelshop test
pnpm --filter @dialogwright/example-wheelshop regress                      # "no changes", then the adversarial suite (the agent family) and the summary
pnpm --filter @dialogwright/example-wheelshop regress --scenario <id>      # one scripted call, turn by turn (--corpus <id>, --adversarial <id>)
pnpm policy:matrix apps/wheelshop && pnpm policy:card apps/wheelshop && pnpm app:diagram apps/wheelshop   # after a change to business.yaml or a tool; read each diff
pnpm agent:bakeoff --cases apps/wheelshop/fixtures/agent/cases.yaml --models inception:mercury-3:instant:realtime --examples on --repeat 3 --judge-ms 150 --replay   # the bake-off's table, from its recording
```

## Rules for this app

- A change to what the shop does, claims, charges or collects is an edit to `business.yaml`, never to code. After it: `pnpm check`, then the three pages above, read as a change in what the line may do and say, committed with the file.
- The line never says a price: `prices.say` is said whole when a caller asks, and it promises nothing the line has not done (the text asking for a photo is sent only by the request's read-back's yes, and only with the caller's consent, so the price line says what happens once the request is in).
- A caller is texted only with their consent: `textOk`, read back, and the gate's `when` on `send_photo_link`. Never send a caller a text from a tool without such a rule, and never check a tool's consent in code. `onText` and the shop page have no gate: the thank-you, the quote and the booking's text go only to the number on file of a request that said yes to texts (matched exactly, `+1` numbers only), the page texts through its allow-list sender (`allowListTexts`), and a reply is read by `ctx.decide`, only for a waiting quote, never by matching its words. A lapsed quote never books. A real number (the shop's phone, the number texts come from) is a setting, never in the folder. Never put a figure in a claim, the price line or a sample reply.
- The line speaks as the business: "we", "the shop", "Example Wheel Works" (call 44). No line, claim, label, tool description or sample reply names a person, and `business.owner`, `oneOwner` and a `background` line about who does the work stay out, since they push the model toward talking about one.
- A turnaround, a promise or a service may come only from `claims` and `services`. Keep each assumption marked as one until the owner confirms it.
- Every read-back says every field, an optional one too, since a form's `then` sends them all (the caller confirms nothing they never heard): an optional field is said through its `says` (each option in the shop's words, and `unset` for when it is left empty, as `diamondCut` is). A tool follows one form; a form's first `then` tool is its write.
- Never put policy in tool code; never record (`regress --client record` is the author's step, with the author's keys); never put a key in a file; never regenerate the baseline, a snapshot or a page of the read back to make something pass. A `FAIL adversarial` case is a finding to fix in the app or the engine.
- The shop page shows callers' numbers whole and their photos: its route stays `auth: 'console'` (and `localOnly: true`), never public, and nothing of it goes into the chat page. The shop's log holds the same: never in the repository, never written by a test outside a temporary folder.
- Fictional data only: invented names and dealerships, the 555 range, example.com.
