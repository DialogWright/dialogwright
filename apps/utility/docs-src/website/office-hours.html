Office hours and contact

Customer office

Our customer office at 100 Main Street is open Monday through Friday, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and is closed on weekends and public holidays. Outages can be reported by phone at any time, day or night.

Customer Service by phone

Customer Service answers at 555-0100 Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. The same number takes outage reports every day of the year.

By mail
