Seasonal assistance programs

Example Power & Light offers help with energy bills in the hottest and coldest months of the year.

Winter Warmth credit

For the 2026 to 2027 heating season, effective October 1, 2026, households with income at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty guidelines can receive a one-time bill credit of up to 300 dollars. Applications are accepted from October 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027.

Summer Cooling help

From June 1 through September 30, customers aged 65 or older, or with a medical need for cooling, can set up a payment arrangement with no down payment.

How to apply

Apply online at www.example.com/assistance or call 555-0100. You will need proof of household income for the last 30 days.
