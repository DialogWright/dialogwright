Outage credits

Example Power & Light works to restore power as quickly as it safely can. When an outage lasts a long time, residential customers receive a credit on their bill.

Who receives a credit

Residential customers whose power is out for 24 hours or more in a row receive a credit of 25 dollars. The credit is applied automatically to the next bill. There is nothing to apply for.

Longer outages

For each further 12 hours without power, another 10 dollars is credited, up to 75 dollars for one outage.

When no credit is given

No credit is given for planned maintenance outages announced at least 48 hours ahead, or for outages caused by damage to equipment the customer owns.

Questions about a credit

If a credit does not appear on your bill within two billing cycles, call Customer Service at 555-0100.
