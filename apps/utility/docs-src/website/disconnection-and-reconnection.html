Disconnection and reconnection

Before a disconnection

We never disconnect service for nonpayment without notice. We mail a written notice at least 10 days before the date service may be disconnected, and we call the phone on the account at least 2 days before that date.

When we do not disconnect

We do not disconnect service for nonpayment on weekends, on public holidays, or on any day the forecast high is below 32 degrees or above 95 degrees.

Reconnecting service

Once the past-due amount is paid, or a payment arrangement is set up, service is reconnected within 24 hours. A reconnection fee of 30 dollars is added to the next bill. The fee is waived the first time in any 12 months.
