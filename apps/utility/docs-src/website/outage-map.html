Outage map

Where to find the map

The outage map at www.example.com/outages shows current outages, the number of customers affected, and the estimated time power will be restored. It is updated every 15 minutes.

Estimated restoration times

Restoration times are estimates. They are updated as crews reach the site and assess the damage.

If your outage is not on the map

The map shows the outages we know about. If your power is out and your street is not on the map, report it by calling 555-0100 at any time, day or night.
